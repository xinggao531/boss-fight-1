Prologue

The Summer the Sea Boiled

I

The Pact Beneath the Sea

Far beneath the islands, where the seabed turns to old volcanic stone, lies Magma Hollow. Five centuries ago a fire spirit named Pyrrhos agreed to sleep there and keep the island's fire asleep with him. In return, the sea shrine sent him a yearly offering of salt and snow.

II

The Waking

This summer the offerings stopped. The tides turned warm, then hot. The sakura along the coast withered in a single night and ash fell on the shrine like black snow. Deep below, the Scorched Sovereign had woken, and he had forged himself a shield of living flame: the Blazing Aegis.

III

Frost and Tide

Ayaka came from the capital with her blade and her fan. Kokomi came from the coral isles with the tide at her back. Water alone boils against him and frost alone melts, but soak him and then chill him, and even a sovereign freezes. Together, they descend into the Hollow.

The Heroines

Frost and Tide

Cryo · Melee

Ayaka

Shirasagi Himegimi, daughter of Inazuma's Kamisato Clan and the Yashiro Commission

  • 1,200 HP
  • 60 energy burst
  • Key 1

A swordswoman who fights up close. Her plain slashes are physical, so they need her fan's frost to hurt the Aegis and to set up Freeze.

  • ClickJ

    Normal Attack · Frostfan Swordplay

    Three quick slashes and a spinning finish. Hits only land within reach of Pyrrhos; if he is just out of range she takes a short step in, and further away you will see "Too far". Every hit charges her burst.

    • 88 / 100 / 112 / 170 damage
    • Physical
    • Blocked by the Aegis
  • E

    Elemental Skill · Frostfan Gale

    An icy gust leaves her fan and sweeps into Pyrrhos, scattering any fireballs in its path, chilling him with Cryo and dropping energy orbs. For the next 6 seconds her blade is frost-infused: slashes turn icy, deal Cryo damage, hit 15% harder, apply Cryo and chip the Aegis.

    • 300 Cryo
    • 6s frost infusion
    • 12s cooldown
  • Q

    Elemental Burst · Eternal Winter Waltz

    A 3-second blizzard howls around her. It strikes hard as it forms, keeps hitting Pyrrhos while he is inside it and blows away fireballs. While it rages her sword hits 2.2× harder with longer reach, and she takes 40% less damage.

    • 520 + 120 Cryo every 0.2s
    • Sword ×2.2 for 3s
    • 60 energy · 16s cooldown

Play her: let Kokomi soak him, then Frostfan Gale and a frost-infused combo to Freeze him, and cut into the brittle ice.

Rules of the Hollow

Reactions and the Aegis

Frozen

Cryo on a soaked boss, or Hydro on a chilled one, locks him in ice for 3.2s. He can't act, takes 30% more damage, and can't be refrozen for 4s after he thaws.

Melt & Vaporize

He wreathes himself in Pyro whenever he attacks. Cryo on his flames Melts for 1.5× damage; Hydro Vaporizes for 2×.

Armour

His rock hide blocks 20% of plain damage on Normal and 40% on Hard. Reactions pierce half of it.

Blazing Aegis

At 55% and 25% health he raises a fire shield and his health cannot drop. Physical hits are blocked; each Cryo or Hydro hit removes one point (54 / 69 / 90 by difficulty). Breaking it stuns him for 5.5s.

Switching

Press 1 or 2 to swap heroines (0.8s). Cooldowns keep ticking off-field, energy orbs give the benched heroine 60%, and if one falls the other steps in.

Moving

Space jumps over ground attacks, C dashes with a moment of invulnerability, and Shift runs. Dashing and running use stamina.

The Foe

Pyrrhos, the Scorched Sovereign

  1. Fireball VolleyA fan of fireballs, thrown two or three times. Dash or step aside; jumping won't help.
  2. Molten ChargeA red lane marks his path, then he rolls down it and leaves fire behind. Get out of the lane or jump.
  3. EruptionRed circles open under you and burst a second later. Keep moving, or jump as they blow.
  4. Flame NovaA shockwave ring races across the floor. Jump it or dash through it.
  5. Meteor Rain Phase IIMeteors fall on marked circles and leave burning patches. Watch the circles.
  6. Inferno Spiral Phase IIStreams of fire spin out from him. Dash through them with care.
